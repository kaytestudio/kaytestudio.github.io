### What happens when you visit the site

**Hosting.** The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), a service of GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, your browser sends standard technical data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address, the page requested, the date and time, and your browser's user agent. GitHub may log this data for security and to run the service. We do not have access to these logs. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**Fonts.** The site uses the Roboto font from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/). To load it, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Live activity feed.** The home page shows recent commits from our public GitHub repositories. To show them, your browser requests the data directly from the GitHub API (api.github.com), which receives your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement linked above.

The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website and in showing our open-source activity (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

GitHub and Google may process data in the United States. Both companies take part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has found to provide adequate protection.

### Contacting us

The contact form does not send data to our servers. When you press send, it opens your own email app with the message filled in, and nothing is sent until you send it from there. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We keep the correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request, and delete it when it is no longer needed. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry, or steps you have asked us to take before entering into an agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

### Stored in your browser

If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key `theme`, so the site remembers it on your next visit. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
